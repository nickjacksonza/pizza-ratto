# Pizza Ratto brand profile

## Positioning

Pizza Ratto is an independent wood-fired pizza restaurant in Salt River, Cape Town: Italian soul with street-corner attitude. It is handmade, funny, local and a little unruly, but never confusing or hostile.

## Voice

- Short, punchy and specific.
- Warm beneath the bravado.
- Serious about dough, fire, ingredients and timing.
- Never corporate, luxurious, generic or over-explained.

## Visual system

- Dusty pink `#f4b9bd`, tomato red `#c91f2b`, orange `#ff6a1a`, dough cream `#f7ebd0`, near-black `#171313`.
- Oversized condensed poster typography with a practical sans-serif for reading.
- Rough borders, checkerboard accents, sticker labels, rotated paper and cropped photography.
- A light CSS paper grain; no heavy animation or decorative JavaScript.

## Brand characters

Remi is the rat mascot. Big Bertha is the wood-fired oven. The venue connects to vintage Vespa culture, and a mural was painted by local artist Buffy. Any fuller story needs owner approval.
